Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, installation, and support.

How long does installation take?

Most installations are completed within 2-3 business days after your order is confirmed. The actual installation process typically takes 1-2 hours, depending on your home's setup. Our technician will arrive within a scheduled 2-hour window and handle everything from running cables to setting up your Wi-Fi network.

Are there any contracts or cancellation fees?

We believe in keeping things simple and transparent. There are no long-term contracts, and you can cancel your service at any time without penalty. We're confident you'll love our service and want to stay because of the quality we provide, not because you're locked into a contract.

Do I need to be home for installation?

Yes, an adult (18 years or older) needs to be present during the installation appointment. Our technician will need access to both the outside and inside of your property to complete the installation and ensure everything is working correctly. They'll also provide a brief orientation on how to use your new internet service.

What equipment is included with my plan?

All plans include a Wi-Fi router at no additional cost. The specific model varies by plan: Basic plans include a standard dual-band router, Premium plans include an advanced tri-band router, and Ultimate plans include a high-performance mesh Wi-Fi system for whole-home coverage. Business plans include business-grade routers with advanced security features.

Do you offer bundle discounts with TV or phone service?

While we specialize in providing top-quality internet service, we do offer partnerships with select streaming TV providers and VoIP phone services. Customers who add these services through our partners can receive discounts of up to 15% on their monthly bill. Ask our customer service team about current bundle offers when you sign up.

How do I troubleshoot connection issues?

If you experience connection issues, try these steps: 1) Restart your router by unplugging it for 30 seconds, then plugging it back in. 2) Check if other devices are also experiencing issues. 3) Test your connection using our speed test tool on our website. 4) Ensure your device is within good range of your Wi-Fi router. If issues persist, contact our 24/7 technical support team who can remotely diagnose your connection and send a technician if necessary.
